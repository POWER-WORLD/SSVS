How Scoring Works

SSVS unifies algorithmic coding statistics, version control metrics, and university CGPA into a normalized multi-dimensional score.

Benchmarking:
Achieving a benchmark target (e.g. 300 LeetCode problems or 1800 rating) grants 100% of the metric's allocated max marks.
Negative Backlog Safeguard:
Active backlogs deduct penalty points directly from candidate subtotals before scaling, preventing unfair advantages.
Excellence Bonuses:
Surpassing bonus thresholds grants bonus points awarded transparently without double-counting.
